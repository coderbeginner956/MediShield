MODEL_TASK=classification
MODEL_DEVICE=cpu

Mandatory Terminology Guide

AI visual screening: Model-generated feature classification or anomaly score.

Reference comparison: Keypoint and structural difference between specimen and reference.

Label inspection: OCR-extracted typography and date logic validation.

Record verification: Cross-check against internal product & batch database.

Confirmed counterfeit: ONLY when backed by authoritative external laboratory assay or manufacturer confirmation.
